# 20261029100000_hr_disputes

Compliance disputes (BRD 5.2 B4, §6.8, N12, TC14; DEV-449). A technician, or a supervisor or coordinator for
them, disputes one missed check-in with a reason, up to the cut-off day (the 25th). A named reviewer
(Settings → Reporting → Dispute reviewers) accepts it, which waives the miss, or rejects it with a reason.

- `hr.disputes`: one per missed event; who raised it, when, and whether for the technician.
- `hr.dispute_decisions`: one per dispute; accepted with a waiver reason, or rejected with a note.
- `hr.waivers.dispute_id`: the waiver an accepted dispute made.
- `hr.raise_dispute`, `hr.decide_dispute`: the only ways in, one transaction each.
- `hr.finalize_month` replaced: same signature, now refused while a dispute on the month is undecided.

Live-safe: schema `hr` is read only by this branch. One nullable column on `hr.waivers`. Idempotent.
Nothing to expose (`hr` is exposed since row 10).
