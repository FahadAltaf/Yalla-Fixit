# 20261021110000_finance_queue_list

One read-only view, `finance.queue_list`: each queue item with its work order and quotation numbers, client,
the quotation's owner (shown as coordinator until D-18), assignee, and the completion % as BRD §6.1 defines
it, (earlier invoiced batches + this item) ÷ quotation total. No data changes; nothing on live `main` reads it.
Needs `20261021100000`.

Tested on the harness: Q-1042's three items read 41.7 %, 75.0 % and 100 % in the list, and keep those values
after later batches are invoiced; an item without a quotation shows no %; server-only.
